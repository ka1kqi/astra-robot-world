      What the system can actually promise

      The evaluator defines success. A model's explanation does not certify it.

      Learning means bounded program search

      Review a measurable goal, capture a scene snapshot, test a candidate, inspect failures, and revise. A successful candidate runs three times from fresh copies before it can be saved. The draft has 3–10 trials, including confirmations.

      Goals are topple, displace, circle, and extract. This is task-specific program search with persistent notes; it does not train model weights or execute arbitrary generated Python.

      Per model turn: at most 36 experiment/read-only calls and 12 live calls, then a tool-free summary. The experiment budget includes notebook writes and saving actions; its name does not mean every call is read-only.

      MotionStep operations live inside programs

      move_to_posegrippercontact_strokewaitpick_place

      These are nested ActionProgram.steps operations, not five extra Astra tools. pick_place also exists as a separate top-level tool with its own argument schema.

      Programs have at most 24 steps and 30 simulated seconds. The action controller enforces a 40 N aggregate robot contact-load limit, joint limits, and collision rules.

      Extraction has three distinct object roles

      object_id is the lower object to extract; supported_id is the upper object resting on it; landing_id is the body that must support the upper afterward. Do not substitute the topple goal's support_id.

      The lower must move far enough from its start and horizontally away from the upper. The upper must lose lower support and land on the specified body; both must settle. Direct upper manipulation is forbidden. Other bodies, including the landing body, are preserved. Supporting contact is measured; full tray containment is not.

      A clear approach is useful evidence, not success

      check_approaches checks up to 8 candidate routes of up to 8 absolute world-coordinate waypoints on copies. It searches IK and samples joint interpolation for collisions. It consumes no physical trial and does not move the live world.

      IK's alternate seeds are mathematical starting guesses on scratch data. They do not teleport the live arm. A failed bounded search does not prove geometric impossibility; a clear kinematic path does not establish a successful physical grasp or contact action.
